There is a difference between knowing many interfaces and understanding a system well enough to notice when something is wrong.

I am trying to spend more time on the second kind of knowledge: boundaries, failure modes, data flow, ownership, and the assumptions that sit underneath an abstraction.

That changes how I use AI tools as well. The useful question is less *can it write this for me?* and more *can I specify the contract, review the result, and explain why the design should survive contact with reality?*

This is slower at first. It also seems to compound better.
