I like ambitious work, but I do not want every hour of life to feel like a dashboard.

Some of the better ideas arrive after the obvious work is finished: while walking, looking through old photographs, reading outside the immediate problem, or simply doing nothing useful for a while.

I am trying to protect more of that space.
