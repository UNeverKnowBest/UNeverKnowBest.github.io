# Rainy evening — design contract

One continuous evening: distance → reflection → connection. Home contains Hero,
About, and Connect. CV carries the academic detail in the same visual language.

Keep cinematic rain, desaturated blue-green foliage, wet reflections, editorial
serifs, generous margins, fine rules, and restrained warm light. About is an
integrated mist-lit environment, not a floating white card. Connect returns to a
dark scene with a small suggestion of life behind warm windows.

The supplied concept in `assert/` guides mood and hierarchy. The three environment
assets are adapted from that reference with all interface lettering removed and
overt Japanese signage/props reduced. No film frame tracing or recognizable film
characters. No neon, glass cards, decorative Japanese type, custom cursors,
magnetic buttons, or spring/bounce effects.

Scroll remains native. Backgrounds crossfade over an opaque foundation. Text
gently enters and recedes in both directions. Mobile reduces blur, parallax, and
rain density. Reduced motion disables rain, interpolation, transforms, and blur.
All copy remains accessible without JavaScript. Long content grows naturally.

Never fabricate contact destinations. Unconfigured contact rows are noninteractive.
